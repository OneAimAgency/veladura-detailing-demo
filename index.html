    Miami
    questions.

    Does the Miami sun really damage paint and leather?

    Constant UV and heat fade paint, dry out leather and crack plastics over time. Regular washes, UV protection inside, a coating or film outside, and ceramic tint all help slow it down.

    What are the white spots on my car?

    Usually water spots: minerals left when sprinkler or hard water dries in the sun. Fresh ones wash off; older ones need the water-spot removal add-on, and some need polishing.

    How soon should lovebugs come off?

    Within a day or two if you can. Fresh ones rinse off easily. Left in the Florida sun, the residue dries and bakes onto the finish, and it gets much harder to remove without marking the clear coat. They swarm twice a year, typically late spring and late summer.

    Can you wash my car in my condo garage?

    Yes. Where hoses aren't allowed, we use a low-water or waterless method and coordinate access with your building.

    What should I do before hurricane season?

    Wash and protect the paint before the car sits, clear the drains and cowl, and store it covered if you can. The storage-prep add-on handles this.

    How dark can my window tint be?

    Florida sets legal limits for tint darkness, and they differ by window. We'll show you the legal options for your car before you choose.

    How long will you have my car?

    A wash takes an hour or two; details take a few hours; correction, coating and film take one to several days. You'll get the time with your confirmation.
